# Topic 1 Research Notes: Credit Scoring

## Research question

This topic studies credit scoring and model reliability in financial risk assessment.

## Data plan

The raw data will be stored in `data/raw/`. Cleaned model-ready data will be stored in `data/processed/`.

## Method plan

The first step is to build baseline models and check whether the model outputs are stable and interpretable.

## Claim boundary

Fairness diagnostics or subgroup residual checks should not be interpreted as legal fairness proof or regulatory compliance evidence.
