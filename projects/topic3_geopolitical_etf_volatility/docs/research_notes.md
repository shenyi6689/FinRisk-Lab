# Topic 3 Research Notes: Geopolitical ETF Volatility

## Research question

This topic studies whether geopolitical events are associated with changes in ETF volatility.

## Data plan

The project will use a geopolitical event list and ETF market data.

## Method plan

The first step is to construct event windows and compare volatility around event dates.

## Claim boundary

Event-window volatility evidence does not prove strict causal identification. It should be interpreted as association-based evidence.
